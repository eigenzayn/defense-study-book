Most of the cost goes into the flow, not into the temperature we want.

569minsolver time of the reference simulation for about ten minutes of flow in a 75 mm tank

In each time step the solver couples velocity and pressure again, even after the flow has settled into its pattern. The temperature it carries changes far more slowly.

The engineering question

Can we keep the temperature physics and stop paying for the flow every time?

The answer depends on whether the temperature changes the flow. That is what my thesis tests.

Before any industrial use, these points must be shown on the process's own physics.

  • Reference data per operating regime, and what it costs to produce
  • Coverage: does the recording contain the motions the process will see?
  • Conservation and boundedness of every correction
  • Error on the quantity the process cares about, not only on a test tank
  • Comparison with measurements (a lab-rig study is running)
  • Physics my tank does not have: turbulence, several phases, reaction heat
How I work
  1. Follow the energy budget until the error has a location.
  2. Fix it within physical bounds: conservative, no new extremes.
  3. State where the method stops, with numbers.
