The question

Most of the cost goes into the flow, not into the temperature we want.

569minsolver time for about ten minutes of flow in a 75 mm tank

Can we keep the temperature physics, and stop paying for the flow every time?

It depends on one thing: does the temperature change the flow?

The idea · recurrence CFD

Record the flow once, replay it for the temperature, about 260 times cheaper.

The catch: the flow is fixed in the recording, so the temperature can no longer change it.

Limits · cooled lid

A recording only serves the flow regime it was made in.

cold plumes under a cooled lid: motion that the insulated recording never saw
0.06Kend error of the heat content with the correction, on 9.62 K of cooling

But the layers separate too slowly while the tank fills. That is missing transport, and no local fix can supply it.

Backup · cost

The 260 times is solver time against solver time, without the recording.

  • Reference: 569 min for 628 s of flow, image output removed
  • Replay: 0.81–4.73 min, mean 2.16 min for 600 s; range 120–700x across mixing strengths
  • The recording is one full CFD run; the saving starts with the second replay

Backup · validation

Before any industrial use, shown on the process's own physics.

  • Reference data per operating regime, and what it costs to produce
  • Coverage: does the recording contain the motions the process will see?
  • Conservation and boundedness of every correction
  • Error on the quantity the process cares about, not only on a test tank
  • Comparison with measurements (a lab-rig study is running)
  • Physics my tank does not have: turbulence, several phases, reaction heat
