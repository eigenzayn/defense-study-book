The test case

A warm jet builds a front only five millimetres thick.

  • Warm inflow at mid-height for 315 s, then cold inflow until 600 s
  • Laminar: Reynolds number about 100, no turbulence model
  • Closed tank, 75 mm: every joule in and out is accounted for
  • The outlet sits just below the front

Q2 · the correction of this thesis

A bounded anti-diffusion rebuilds the front without new extremes.

Heat moves uphill, but both cells stay inside their neighbours' range:

Δqf = s · min(Q+h, Q−c)

No new extremes, energy kept face by face, acting across the front.

Limiter in the spirit of flux-corrected transport (Boris & Book 1973; Zalesak 1979).

Evidence and limits

What the evidence supports, and what it does not.

Three things to take home
  1. A measured trade-off in the standard replay, and its cause: a smeared front drains heat through the outlet.
  2. A conservative, bounded anti-diffusive correction that rebuilds the front without new extremes.
  3. The limit of local corrections: what remains is transport, and a recording belongs to its regime.

Backup · derivation (thesis App. D)

Below s = 1/6 no cell can leave its bounds, even before the clamp.

Each face gives at most s·Q+h to the warm cell and takes at most s·Q−c from the cold one.

A cell with nf faces receives at most nf·s·Q+ and gives at most nf·s·Q−.

So −Q− ≤ C·ΔT ≤ Q+ whenever nf·s ≤ 1, i.e. s ≤ 1/6 on the hexahedral mesh.

Above 1/6 the clamp may act; the working range 0.40–0.50 relies on the clamp and the global conservation constraint.
