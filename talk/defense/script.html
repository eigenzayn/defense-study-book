01

Title

A recording can carry heat that drives its own flow, if we correct the error where it is made and record the right regime.

Technical

Picture a small closed tank of cold liquid at two hundred and ninety-three kelvin. A warm jet at three hundred and thirty-three kelvin enters from the side, rises, and builds a warm stratified layer under the lid. Simulating these ten minutes with conventional CFD took five hundred and sixty-nine minutes, more than nine hours. For the through-flow vessel of our laboratory rig, one reference simulation ran seven point four days. Most of that time recomputes a flow that has already settled. Recurrence CFD records the flow once and replays only the heat, but it was built for passive scalars. Here the temperature drives the flow that carries it. My question is how far one recording can carry such a non-passive scalar, and what the replay needs where it cannot. The claim I will defend is that a recording can carry heat that drives its own flow, if we correct the error where it is made and record the right regime.

Casual

Imagine a small closed tank of cold liquid. Through the side wall, a warm jet squirts in, floats upward, and piles up as a warm layer under the lid. Simulating those ten minutes took a computer five hundred and sixty-nine minutes, more than nine hours. For the through-flow vessel in our lab, one reference run took seven point four days. Most of that time goes on recomputing a flow that has long since settled. Recurrence CFD films the flow once and replays only the heat. But it was made for things like dyes, which just ride along, and here the temperature pushes the flow around. So how far can one film carry that, and what must the replay add? My bet: a recording can carry heat that drives its own flow, if we correct the error where it is made and record the right regime.

Inti: Satu rekaman aliran bisa membawa panas yang mendorong alirannya sendiri, asal error dikoreksi di tempat terjadinya dan rezim aliran yang benar direkam. Ucapkan & kenapa: - Tangki 293 K, jet hangat 333 K dari samping → hangat naik dan menumpuk di bawah lid (Bab 3). - CFD biasa butuh 569 menit (tangki) dan 7,4 hari (vessel lab) → waktu habis di coupling velocity-pressure yang dipecahkan ulang tiap step (Bab 2). - Recurrence CFD merekam gerak sekali dan memutar ulang hanya panas → ia dibuat untuk passive scalar, padahal di sini temperatur mendorong aliran lewat buoyancy (Bab 2). - Klaim yang dibela: rekaman cukup bila error dikoreksi di thermocline dan rezim yang benar direkam → ini ringkasan jawaban Q1 sampai Q5 (Bab 9). Kalau ditanya: Is this still recurrence CFD if the temperature acts back on the flow? Not in the strict sense. The replay keeps one direction only, the recorded motion moves the temperature. We measure what this costs and add a correction for what it loses.

Perkenalan. Tangki kecil, jet hangat membangun lapisan. Simulasi penuh 9 jam lebih untuk 10 menit aliran, dan untuk bejana lab satu simulasi referensi lebih dari seminggu. Ide tesis: rekam alirannya sekali, lalu pindahkan panas dengan rekaman itu saja.

02

Two time scales

The flow settles in seconds, the heat takes minutes.

Technical

Start with a hot-water storage tank, a steel ladle, a chemical reactor. In all of them the flow reaches its pattern within seconds. The temperature then evolves over minutes to hours. Conventional CFD keeps solving momentum and pressure at a small time step long after the flow has settled. That is acceptable for one design study. But a digital twin must keep pace with the plant, and comparing operating points needs answers in minutes rather than days. So the aim is simple to state. Compute the flow once, and follow the slow heat cheaply. The flow settles in seconds, the heat takes minutes, and that gap is what a recording can exploit.

Casual

Think of a hot-water tank in a building, a ladle of molten steel, a chemical reactor. In each one, the flow finds its pattern within seconds. The heat then crawls on for minutes or hours. Yet the usual simulation keeps solving the flow at tiny time steps long after nothing new happens. For one design study, fine. But a digital twin has to keep up with the real plant, and comparing operating points should take minutes, not days. So the plan is plain. Work out the flow once, then follow the slow heat cheaply. The flow settles in seconds, the heat takes minutes.

Inti: Aliran cepat settle dan panas lambat, dan celah waktu itulah yang dimanfaatkan rekaman. Ucapkan & kenapa: - Tangki air panas, ladle baja, reaktor → pola aliran terbentuk cepat, temperatur berevolusi jauh lebih lama (premis recurrence CFD, Bab 2). - CFD biasa tetap menyelesaikan momentum dan pressure di time step kecil → biaya terbesar ada di coupling velocity-pressure (Bab 2). - Digital twin harus mengikuti pabrik, dan perbandingan titik operasi butuh menit, bukan hari → ini motivasi praktis (Bab 1). - Hitung aliran sekali, ikuti panas dengan murah → sah karena bila u dibekukan, persamaan energi linear dalam T (Bab 2). Kalau ditanya: Does the separation of time scales hold in your own case? Partly. The filling time of the tank is 169 s, so the heat is slower than the jet. But the flow changes when the inflow turns cold and under the cooled lid, which is why the database must hold the right regime. Banyak proses termal punya dua skala waktu: alirannya stabil dalam hitungan detik, suhunya berkembang dalam menit sampai jam. CFD tetap menghitung aliran di tiap langkah waktu kecil, jadi mahal untuk digital twin atau sapuan banyak titik operasi.

03

The database

Record the motion once, replay the heat many times.

Technical

Here is how the recording works. I run one conventional simulation in ANSYS Fluent and store six hundred frames, one per second. Each frame holds the cell-to-cell shifts, which say where the content of every cell moves in that second. The replay applies these shifts in sequence, adds face swaps between neighbouring cells to represent mixing, and uses a global balance to keep the total heat right. The flow equations are never solved again. In the closed tank, the reference took five hundred and sixty-nine minutes and one replay takes about two. For the laboratory vessel, seven point four days against eight and a half minutes, more than a thousand times faster. The recording itself costs a full simulation, nine days for the vessel, so the saving comes from replaying many times. The price is that the recorded motion is frozen.

Casual

So here is the trick. I run one normal simulation in Fluent and save six hundred frames, one every second. Each frame is like a map of moves, telling where the content of every cell goes in that second. The replay follows those moves one after another. It also lets neighbouring cells swap a little heat across their faces, which stands in for mixing, and a global balance keeps the total heat honest. The flow equations never run again. The closed tank took five hundred and sixty-nine minutes, and one replay takes about two. In the lab vessel it is seven point four days against eight and a half minutes. The film itself costs a full run, nine days, so we win by replaying it many times. The price is that the recorded motion is frozen.

04

The open point

Here the temperature moves the flow that carries it.

Technical

This works well for a dye, a passive scalar that the flow carries without being affected. The reference solves the Navier-Stokes equations with continuity and the energy equation. The density changes by only about one point five per cent over our temperature range. Fluent uses a tabulated density, and this agrees with the Boussinesq form to within the same small fraction, so I write the equations in that form. There the density enters only in the buoyancy term, minus g beta times T minus T zero. That term is absent from the replay. The recorded velocity moves the temperature, but the temperature cannot change the velocity, because the motion is frozen. Two more points are open. The face swap strength, f s d, is a free constant that someone sets by hand. And a cooled lid acts locally on a temperature field that an adiabatic recording never saw. Coupling, mixing and walls are the three gaps that frame the thesis.

Casual

A dye is easy. It rides along with the flow and does not push back. The reference solves the Navier-Stokes equations. Over our temperature range the liquid gets only about one point five per cent lighter or heavier. The solver uses a tabulated density, and that agrees with the simpler form named after Boussinesq, so I write the equations that way. The density then counts in one place only, the buoyancy term. That is warm rises and cold sinks, written as minus g beta times the temperature difference. That term is missing from the replay. The recorded velocity moves the temperature, but the temperature cannot change the velocity. Two loose ends remain. The swap strength f s d is a free number that I set by hand. And a cooled lid draws heat locally from a field that a recording with an insulated lid never saw.

Ucapkan & kenapa: - Dye mudah → passive scalar tidak mempengaruhi aliran (Bab 2). - Density berubah hanya sekitar 1,5 persen → Fluent memakai density tabulasi, dan hasilnya sama dengan bentuk Boussinesq pada pecahan kecil yang sama, jadi density hanya penting di buoyancy term (Bab 2 dan 3). - Term −gβ(T−T0) tidak ada di replay → u rekaman beku, T tidak bisa mengubah u (Bab 2). - f_sd konstanta bebas → difusi tidak bisa diambil dari rekaman, sebab displacement tidak mendifusikan, jadi harus ditambah dengan parameter yang diset tangan (Bab 2). - Lid dingin bekerja lokal pada medan yang tak pernah dilihat rekaman adiabatik → ini diuji di Q3 (Bab 6). Kalau ditanya: Why not add the buoyancy term to the replay? That would need the velocity equation again, and the speed-up would go. The thesis asks how far one can get without it. rCFD dibuat untuk skalar pasif. Suhu di sini tidak pasif: suhu menggerakkan aliran. Replay hanya menyimpan satu arah kopling. Dua pertanyaan lain: konstanta pencampuran bebas, dan dinding dingin yang bekerja lokal.

05

Aim and design

How far can one database carry a non-passive scalar?

Technical

To see how far one database can go, I use a two by two design. Two boundary conditions, an adiabatic lid and a cooled lid. Two replays, standard and extended. Each case is compared with its own CFD reference. With the adiabatic lid, recording and reference are the same flow, so every difference is numerical. The cooled lid adds the physical coupling. Five questions follow. Question one, the effect of the free f s d on accuracy and cost. Question two, whether a bounded correction can undo the smearing. Question three, how much heat leaves through the cooled lid. Question four, which error remains once the heat loss is right. Question five, whether this transfers to the laboratory vessel, with lid feed and floor feed. The tank measures seventy-five by seventy-five by fifteen millimetres, with eighty-five thousand six hundred and twenty-five cells of one millimetre. The jet is laminar at a Reynolds number of about one hundred, so no turbulence model blurs the comparison.

Casual

To test this I built a small grid of cases. Two kinds of lid, one that keeps heat in and one that cools. Two kinds of replay, standard and extended. Every case is judged against its own full simulation. With the insulated lid, the film and the reference are the same flow, so any difference comes from the numbers alone. The cooled lid adds real physics. Five questions. One, what does the free f s d do to accuracy and cost. Two, can a limited correction undo the smearing. Three, how much heat leaves through the lid. Four, what error is left when that is right. Five, does it work in the lab vessel, fed from the lid or from the floor. The tank is seventy-five by seventy-five by fifteen millimetres, with eighty-five thousand six hundred and twenty-five cells. The jet is smooth and laminar, Reynolds number about one hundred, so no turbulence model gets in the way.

06

Measures

Every replay is scored with the same few measures.

Technical

Before the results, here is how every replay is scored, shown on a model tank. T mean says how much heat the tank holds. z cog says how high that heat sits. The heterogeneity, delta T mean, says how strongly the tank is layered. At the end of the run I take the temperature profile on the centre line and compare it with the reference. Its departure, delta of y, is the end profile error. The thermocline thickness w is the distance between the ten and the ninety per cent crossings of the temperature step. For any trace, the error splits as RMSE squared equals bias squared plus sigma e squared, an offset plus an error of shape. With these measures fixed, the standard replay comes first.

Casual

Before any result, here is the ruler. Picture the tank as a model. T mean says how much heat the tank holds, like the total warmth in a bath. z cog says how high that warmth sits. Delta T mean says how strongly the water is layered, a smooth bath or a layer cake. At the end I look at the temperature along the centre line and measure how far it departs from the reference. That departure is delta of y. The thermocline thickness w is the band where warm turns to cold, measured between the ten and the ninety per cent marks. The error of any trace splits in two. Bias squared is a constant offset, and sigma e squared is the wiggle of shape around it. Now the standard replay.

Ucapkan & kenapa: - T mean, z cog, δT mean → panas total, ketinggian panas, dan seberapa berlapis tangki (Bab 4). - Profil garis tengah di akhir dan δ(y) → memeriksa bentuk thermocline, yang tidak terlihat dari integral (Bab 4). - w dari 10 sampai 90 persen langkah temperatur → definisi tebal thermocline mengikuti Musser (1998) (Bab 4). - RMSE² = bias² + σe² → memisahkan offset dari galat bentuk, dan di Q1 hampir seluruh error adalah bentuk (Bab 5). - Satu ukuran integral saja menyesatkan → trace bisa bagus dengan thermocline terlalu tajam (Bab 5). Kalau ditanya: Why report the thickness and not only the RMSE? Ukuran galat: T_mean (jumlah panas), z_cog (tinggi panas), heterogenitas, profil akhir dan ketebalan termoklin w 10-90 %. RMSE terbagi menjadi bias (offset) dan sigma (bentuk).

07

Standard replay

No single face-exchange strength serves both error measures.

Technical

Question one. I replayed the adiabatic tank with seven values of f s d. The mean temperature is best with little or no added mixing, with an error of about one kelvin. The stratification height is best near f s d equal to zero point five. No single face exchange strength serves both measures. Figure five point one shows the pattern in the traces. Stronger mixing makes the replay too warm during the inflow and too cold after it. The cause sits at the thermocline. The outlet lies just below it. After the inflow, a smeared thermocline puts warm liquid at the outlet, and the tank loses heat. During the inflow the same overlap makes the replay keep too much heat. A numerical error at the thermocline becomes an error in the energy balance. The recorded motion is frozen, and face swaps alone cannot reach the real problem.

Casual

First question. I tried seven values of f s d on the insulated tank. For the mean temperature, little or no extra mixing wins, with an error of about one kelvin. For the height of the warm layer, a value near zero point five wins. So no single setting wins both. The traces on the slide show why. With stronger mixing the replay is too warm while the jet flows in, and too cold after it stops. The cause sits at the thermocline, the thin band where warm meets cold. The outlet is just below it. Once the jet stops, a smeared band puts warm liquid at the outlet, and it leaves the tank. While the jet runs, the same overlap makes the tank keep too much heat. A small error in shape turns into an error in the energy. The motion is frozen, and swaps alone cannot reach this.

08

Extended replay

A bounded anti-diffusive step sharpens the thermocline.

Technical

Face swaps can only smooth. Once a thermocline is smeared, no value of f s d rebuilds it. So I add a step inside each replay step, after the face swaps and before the global balance. Heat moves from a hot cell to a cold neighbour up the local gradient, which is diffusion run backwards. The amount is a share s of the smaller of two headrooms, Q h plus and Q c minus, local bounds of Zalesak type. A vertical weight, the squared cosine between face direction and vertical, directs the exchange across the thermocline. With it the step is bounded and conservative by construction for s up to one half. Alone it is unstable, and the values leave the data range, as the left panel shows. With the limiter the front sharpens from about nine cells to one. In the tank the recorded diffusivity saturates the cap, so every gain comes from the correction.

Casual

Face swaps only smooth, like stirring milk into tea. Once the thermocline is smeared, no f s d sharpens it again. So I add one more step inside each replay step, after the face swaps and before the global balance. It moves heat from a warm cell to a cold neighbour up the local gradient, which is diffusion run backwards. The amount is a share s of the smaller of two allowances. Q h plus is what the warm cell can give, Q c minus what the cold cell can take, within the bounds of its neighbours, after Zalesak. A vertical weight sends the exchange across the band, and up to s equal to one half it stays bounded and conserves heat. Without the limiter it blows up. With it, a front nine cells wide sharpens to one. In this tank the recorded diffusivity is already at the cap, so every gain is from the correction.

Ucapkan & kenapa

Inti: Satu langkah anti-diffusive yang dibatasi limiter menajamkan thermocline tanpa melanggar batas data. Ucapkan & kenapa: - Face swaps hanya menghaluskan → pertukarannya isotropik dan tidak tahu stratifikasi, jadi melebarkan thermocline (Bab 5). - Langkah baru dipasang setelah face swaps dan sebelum global balance → urutan komposisi adalah bagian dari metode (Bab 2, operator splitting). - Panas pindah dari sel hangat ke sel dingin naik gradien, sebesar s kali yang lebih kecil dari Q_h+ dan Q_c− → batas lokal ala Zalesak, jadi tidak ada ekstrem baru dan panas terkonservasi (Bab 4). - Bobot cos² vertikal → thermocline hampir horizontal, jadi pertukaran harus melintasinya, dan jumlah cos² enam sisi sama dengan 2 sehingga syarat menjadi s ≤ 1/2 (Bab 4). - Model 1D: front 8,8 sel jadi 0,9 sel, tanpa limiter gangguan 1 mK membesar (Bab 4). Kalau ditanya: Is anti-diffusion not just an artificial sharpener? It is bounded by the local extrema of the neighbours and conservative, so it creates no new values. It only undoes the smearing that the face exchange adds.

Koreksi setelah tiap langkah memindahkan panas melawan gradien sehingga front menajam. Tanpa batas, nilainya keluar rentang data. Dengan limiter gaya FCT, tiap sel tetap dalam rentang tetangganya. Bobot vertikal, panas total tetap.

Q2 evidence

Both errors fall as the corrected thermocline approaches the reference thickness.

Technical

You may ask why s is zero point four five on the last slide. Figure five point six collects all correction runs at the end of the run. Panel a shows the trace error of the mean temperature against thermocline thickness, panel b the end profile error. The dashed line is the reference thickness, and the open circle is the standard replay. The vertically weighted runs fall towards the reference thickness, and both errors fall with them. The unweighted form reaches the reference thickness only at s equal to zero point nine, far outside its guaranteed range, with grid-scale oscillations. Past the reference thickness the trace error can keep falling while the end profile worsens, so no single strength minimises all measures. Panel c plots the two errors against each other with the Pareto front. I therefore define the working point as a range, s from zero point four to zero point five.

Cooled lid

The database must be recorded under cooling.

Technical

Now the cooled lid. Heat leaves through the lid only as fast as the flow brings warm liquid up to it. The wall term acts on the top cell row, q equals h times A times T minus T ambient, with h equal to one hundred watts per square metre kelvin and T ambient equal to two hundred and ninety-three kelvin. Cooled liquid becomes denser, sinks, and is replaced. That is convection. Replay and reference must simulate the same flow and be compared at the same instant. A database recorded with an adiabatic lid never contains this sinking motion. The replay then removes only sixty-three per cent of the reference lid heat loss, and neither more mixing nor the correction recovers the rest. A database recorded under cooling gives ninety-eight per cent with the standard replay. With the correction the bar reaches one hundred and two per cent, thirty-four joules more than the reference out of about sixteen hundred. The wall term removes h A times T minus T ambient, so it depends on the temperature right under the lid. The standard replay leaves the top layer too cold, and the correction moves heat up into it, so the lid sees warmer liquid and removes a little more. The next slide shows that the end mean stays close to the reference. A boundary condition can switch on a new flow regime, and the database must contain it.

Where learning fits

The two remaining errors are candidates for data-driven models.

Technical

Two errors remain, and both are candidates for data-driven models. The correction strength is still chosen by sweeps for each case, and the replay cannot create motion that is missing from the database. A recurrence database holds many states of one flow, which makes a natural training set, and a trained model is cheap to run. A first step is a small network that predicts the local correction strength from the replayed field, acting inside the limiter so that bounds and conservation stay. Further options are a neural operator that maps one field to the next, and a sequence model such as a transformer or xLSTM that chooses which frame or database to play next. None of these was tried in this thesis. For a digital twin, the replay is fast and can be held to sensor data by nudging, as in Figure four point seven. It still needs a database for every regime the plant can enter, and a correction strength that does not depend on a reference.
