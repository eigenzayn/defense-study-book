01

Title

The thesis speeds up thermal flow simulation with recurrence CFD, first in a simple closed configuration and then in the laboratory vessel.

Script

Good morning, and thank you for coming. My thesis is called Data-based Numerical Simulation of Buoyant Jet Flow. It investigates how recurrence CFD can speed up thermal flow simulations, when the temperature is an active, non-passive scalar that drives the flow through buoyancy. I structured the work in two stages. First, I deliberately use a simplified numerical configuration, a small closed configuration with laminar flow. It gives a clean, controlled setting. There I can isolate the numerical errors of the replay, develop the bounded thermocline correction, and test the thermal boundary conditions, without extra uncertainty from turbulence modelling or a complex geometry. Then, in the second stage, I transfer these operators to a practical application, the 7.5 litre laboratory through-flow vessel. To begin the story, the next slide shows why a recording of the flow is worth having at all.

Ucapkan & kenapa

Slide ini hanya judul dan animasi, tapi pembukanya diucapkan di sini. Perkenalkan topik besar: mempercepat simulasi aliran termal dengan recurrence CFD, saat suhu ikut menggerakkan aliran lewat gaya apung. Lalu strategi dua tahap: konfigurasi tertutup sederhana dan laminar untuk mengisolasi galat dan mengembangkan koreksi, baru kemudian vessel laboratorium 7,5 liter.

Buka dengan judul lalu langsung artinya dalam satu kalimat: saya mensimulasikan jet hangat yang naik di cairan dingin tanpa menghitung ulang alirannya, karena aliran itu diambil dari data satu run solver. 02

Two time scales

The flow settles in seconds and the temperature takes minutes, so the flow is worth storing and the temperature worth following.

Script

Think of a hot-water store, a steel ladle or a chemical reactor. The flow settles within seconds. The temperature needs minutes to spread. Conventional CFD, the usual flow simulation, ignores this difference. It keeps solving the momentum and pressure equations at every step, even when the flow hardly changes. A digital twin, a live model that follows a running plant, needs answers within minutes. This is why the fast flow is worth storing and the slow temperature is worth following. Recurrence CFD does exactly that. It records the flow once, and plays it back many times.

Ucapkan & kenapa

Setelah judul, jelaskan artinya dalam satu kalimat: jet hangat naik di cairan dingin tanpa menghitung ulang aliran, karena aliran diambil dari data satu run solver. Beri peta singkat empat bagian: motivasi dan metode, konfigurasi tertutup, bejana lab, lalu makna. Di sistem penyimpan panas, ladle baja, atau reaktor kimia, aliran tenang dalam detik tetapi suhu berubah dalam menit. CFD biasa tetap menghitung aliran di setiap langkah, jadi aliran direkam sekali dan diputar ulang.

Di kasus ini jet melintasi domain dalam sekitar 4 detik, tetapi pengisiannya butuh 169 detik. CFD biasa tetap menghitung aliran di setiap langkah, lima langkah per detik dengan tiga puluh iterasi dalam. Digital twin dan sapuan jadwal butuh jawaban dalam menit, jadi aliran disimpan dan suhu diikuti.

03

The database

One solver run is stored and replayed in minutes, but the replay remembers how the flow moved and forgets why.

Script

The recording starts with one conventional CFD simulation. I run it once in ANSYS Fluent. I store the flow as a map for every second, which tells each cell where its contents move next. The replay plays these maps back in order and carries the temperature along with them. Neighbouring cells also exchange a little heat, to mimic mixing, and a global balance keeps the total heat right. The replay is then much faster. The closed configuration drops from about nine and a half hours to about 2 minutes. For the laboratory vessel, days become minutes, about 1250 times faster. But the recorded motion is frozen. The replay remembers how the flow moved, but not why. That missing why is buoyancy, and the next slide turns to it.

04

The open point

Warm liquid expands and rises, the reference keeps this buoyancy and the replay does not, which leaves three weak points and the central question.

Script

To see what the frozen replay misses, we turn to buoyancy. Temperature is not a passive tracer, because warm liquid expands and becomes lighter. Warm the liquid by 40 kelvin, and its density drops by only about 1.5 per cent. Still, that small difference makes the warm liquid rise. This small change allows the Boussinesq approximation. It treats the density as constant everywhere, except in the buoyancy term of the momentum equation. The reference simulation solves the flow and the temperature together. The replay keeps only the temperature equation, driven by the recorded velocity. So the temperature can never push back on the flow. This leaves three weak points. The coupling runs one way. The face-exchange strength f s d, which sets how strongly neighbouring cells mix, is a constant that I set by hand. A cooled lid acts locally, while the recorded flow stays fixed. Together they form the central question, whether a recorded flow can still carry a temperature that drives the flow. The next slide sets up the test.

Ucapkan & kenapa

Replay beku melewatkan fisika apung. Cairan hangat memuai: dengan 40 K, densitas turun dari 998,2 ke 983,2 kg/m3 (sekitar 1,5 persen), gaya apung sekitar 0,15 m/s2. Itu membolehkan Boussinesq, tetapi replay hanya memakai persamaan energi, jadi kopling satu arah. Tiga titik lemah (kopling satu arah, f sd tangan, lid dingin) membentuk pertanyaan pusat tesis.

Cairan hangat memuai: massa sama mengisi ruang lebih besar, densitas turun dari 998,2 ke 983,2 kg/m³. Paket hangat lebih ringan dari cairan dingin yang didesaknya, jadi gravitasi menyisakan gaya ke atas sekitar 0,15 m/s² (1,5 % dari g). Perubahan sekecil ini membolehkan Boussinesq. Referensi menyelesaikan tiga persamaan bersama, replay hanya persamaan energi dengan kecepatan rekaman, jadi kopling satu arah. Dari situ tiga titik lemah.

05

Aim and design

The closed configuration is a warm jet at 333 K entering cold liquid at 293 K, studied in a two-by-two design with five questions.

Script

With the question set, the case comes next. The liquid in the closed configuration rests at 293 kelvin. A warm jet at 333 kelvin enters from the side, and the warm liquid gathers under the lid. The thin border between warm and cold liquid is called the thermocline. The jet crosses the box in seconds, but filling takes minutes, the two clocks from before. A two-by-two design compares each case with its own CFD reference. The lid is insulated or cooled, and the replay is standard or extended, which adds a correction step. With the insulated lid, every error is numerical. The cooled lid adds a physical one. The box is small, and the flow is laminar, at a Reynolds number of about 100. Five questions climb from simple to realistic. Only the fifth leaves the closed configuration for the vessel. Before the answers, the measures need a definition.

06

Measures

Five measures give one common ruler, because one number can hide a large error somewhere else.

Script

Every replay is scored with the same five measures. The mean temperature tells how much heat the box holds. The height of the heat, called z c o g, tells where that heat sits. The heterogeneity, delta T mean, tells how strongly the liquid is layered. The end profile compares temperature against height at the end of the run. The thermocline thickness is the distance between the 10 and 90 per cent temperature levels. It tells how sharp the layer is. I use all five, because one number can hide a large error elsewhere. On a time trace, I split the error into a constant offset and a difference in shape. With this ruler in hand, the standard replay comes first.

Ucapkan & kenapa

Bab 4, alat ukur. Rapor penilaian yang sama untuk semua replay: tiga ukuran global (suhu rata-rata = total panas, tinggi pusat panas = di mana panas berada, heterogenitas delta T mean = seberapa kuat berlapis), lalu profil akhir dan tebal termoklin 10 sampai 90 persen. Galat jejak waktu dipecah menjadi bias kuadrat (geseran konstan) dan varians (beda bentuk).

Semua replay dinilai dengan lima ukuran yang sama, yaitu suhu rata-rata, tinggi pusat panas, heterogenitas, profil akhir, dan tebal termoklin. Satu angka bisa menyembunyikan galat di tempat lain. Galat pada jejak waktu dipecah menjadi bias kuadrat ditambah varians.

07

Standard replay

No setting of the mixing strength satisfies both errors, because the outlet sits just below the thermocline.

Script

Chapter 5 starts with the adiabatic case. Here the standard replay smeared the thermocline, so my first suspect was the mixing. I tested seven values of f s d. The mean temperature was best at low f s d, but the height of the heat was best near 0.5. So no single face-exchange strength satisfies both measures. The reason sits at the outlet, just below the thermocline. The replay's own mixing thickens the thermocline. Warm liquid then reaches the outlet and leaves. So stronger mixing makes the replay too warm during the inflow, and too cold after it. As a result, a local blur becomes a global error in the energy balance. A dial that blurs the whole field cannot fix this.

Ucapkan & kenapa

Bab 5, kasus adiabatik. Termoklin replay standar kabur, jadi tebakan pertama adalah pencampuran. Tujuh nilai f sd: suhu rata-rata terbaik di f sd rendah (galat sekitar 1 K), tinggi panas terbaik dekat 0,5. Sebabnya outlet persis di bawah termoklin, sehingga replay terlalu hangat saat inflow dan terlalu dingin sesudahnya. Jadi perlu langkah yang bekerja di termoklin.

Termoklin replay standar kabur, dan tebakan pertama saya adalah pencampuran. Tujuh nilai f sd diuji, dan dua galat tidak sepakat pada nilai terbaik. Penyebabnya outlet yang tepat di bawah termoklin, sehingga difusi numerik menaikkan panas yang keluar.

08

Extended replay

A bounded step moves temperature up the gradient but never past what the neighbours allow.

Script

This is why the method adds a bounded anti-diffusive correction. Face exchange can only smooth the field. So the correction acts inside every replay step, right after the face exchange. It moves heat up the local gradient, from a colder cell towards a hotter neighbour. It never moves heat past the values of the neighbouring cells. It is like sharpening a blurred photograph without inventing a colour that no neighbour has. Pushing heat uphill can become unstable. So a limiter, a rule of the Zalesak type, caps every exchange. The correction strength s sets how hard the correction pushes. I also weight the exchange by the squared cosine of the angle to the vertical, so it acts across the thermocline. The exchange conserves heat, and it stays inside the bound for s up to one half. On a model thermocline, the correction thins the layer from 8.8 cells to 0.9 cells. It does so without touching the frozen flow, and the replayed field is the real test.

langkah anti-difusiflimiter tipe Zalesakkekuatan koreksi sbobot cos2s sampai satu per dua60 penerapan pada termoklin model

Ucapkan & kenapa

Pertukaran muka hanya bisa memburamkan, jadi langkah koreksi anti-difusi ditambahkan di dalam tiap langkah replay: panas dipindah dari sel dingin ke tetangga yang lebih panas, tidak melewati batas tetangga. Limiter tipe Zalesak, kekuatan s (kekal dan terbatas sampai 0,5), bobot cosinus kuadrat terhadap vertikal. Pada termoklin model, 60 penerapan menipiskan 8,8 sel jadi 0,9 sel.

Di dalam tiap langkah replay, setelah pertukaran muka sel, panas digeser naik mengikuti gradien lokal dengan batas dari sel tetangga. Limiter tipe Zalesak menjaga panas tetap kekal dan terbatas untuk s sampai satu per dua. Bobot arah memusatkan koreksi melintasi termoklin.

Q2 evidence

Both errors fall as the thickness approaches the reference, so a thin thermocline goes with a small error.

Script

To justify the correction strength, I plotted every correction run against its thermocline thickness. One panel shows the error of the mean-temperature trace. The other shows the error of the end profile. The dashed line marks the reference thickness. With the vertical weight, both errors fall together as the thickness approaches the reference. Without it, the reference thickness is reached only at s equal to 0.9, far beyond the safe bound. There the field shows grid-scale oscillations. The two errors trade against each other along a Pareto front, and the thickness comes closest to the reference for s between 0.4 and 0.5. The next step is to cool the lid.

11

Cooled lid

A database recorded under an adiabatic lid gives 63 percent of the heat loss, and one recorded under cooling gives 98 percent.

Script

Chapter 6 cools the lid. The lid removes heat in proportion to the difference between the cell temperature and the ambient temperature. The cooled liquid becomes denser and sinks. So the cooling changes the flow itself. My first replay used the database recorded under the insulated lid. It removed only 63 per cent of the reference heat loss. The reason is that this recording contains no sinking motion. So I recorded the database again, this time under cooling. The standard replay then removed 98 per cent of the heat loss, and the extended replay 102 per cent. A database has to hold the flow regime it is asked to replay. The next slide looks at where the heat sits.

Raising the strength step by step, the departure is smallest at s = 0.55 with 0.22 K, just above the bound of one half.

Script

14

What stays open

The 2.25 K filling error falls to 0.45 K only when a recorded flux is added, which points to missing transport.

Script

The fourth question is about this remaining error. It shows up during the filling, while the warm inflow is active. The layers of the replay separate too slowly. The heterogeneity error reaches 2.25 kelvin, and no local rule changed it. As a diagnostic test, I added the recorded vertical convective flux of the reference run to the replay. This flux is the heat that the moving liquid carries upward. The error then dropped from 2.25 to 0.45 kelvin, without any fitted parameter. So the layers were missing a transport that the replay does not have. This flux is valid only for the run it came from, so it is not part of the method. With the closed configuration complete, the last question moves to the laboratory vessel.

Pertanyaan keempat, galat yang tersisa. Saat inflow hangat lapisan replay memisah terlalu lambat, selisih heterogenitas 2,25 K, tak bisa diperbaiki aturan lokal. Fluks konveksi vertikal dari run referensi (s = 0,475) menurunkannya ke 0,45 K tanpa parameter yang difit, jadi sisa galat adalah transpor yang hilang. Fluks itu hanya uji. Konfigurasi tertutup selesai, lanjut ke bejana.

Saat inflow hangat, lapisan replay terpisah terlalu lambat dan galat heterogenitas mencapai 2,25 K. Fluks konveksi vertikal dari run referensi menurunkannya menjadi 0,45 K pada s = 0,475. Fluks itu hanya uji diagnostik yang berlaku untuk run asalnya, lalu saya beralih ke bejana.

15

Laboratory vessel

The operators transfer to the laboratory vessel, but one setting does not fit both feeds.

Script

Chapter 7 tests whether all of this holds in a real rig. The laboratory vessel is a through-flow cylinder that holds 7.5 litres. A warm pulse enters through the lid or through the floor. Thermocouples measure the temperature at seven heights. I replay both feeds from one database, recorded with the lid feed. That database contains no floor plume. No replay can invent a flow structure that was never recorded. The standard replay of the floor feed needed the largest mixing strength I tested, f s d equal to 160. So the operators, the correction steps, transfer, but a single setting does not fit both feeds. The lid feed uses a weak correction with down-gradient mixing under the lid, which moves heat from warm to cold with a cap. The floor feed uses convective adjustment, which re-orders unstable layers of warm liquid under cold. I chose the settings for each feed separately.

Denyut hangat dari tutup atau dasar, tetapi hanya SATU database umpan-tutup dipakai, sehingga plume dasar tak ada di rekaman. Standar umpan-dasar butuh f sd = 160. Operator berpindah, setelan dipilih terpisah: down-gradient mixing berbatas 0,5 K untuk umpan-tutup, convective adjustment (laju s dan m) untuk umpan-dasar.

16

Q5 evidence

With the floor feed the extended replay keeps 0.61 K of the 1.14 K heterogeneity, about half, where the standard keeps 0.05 K.

Script

The results show the lid feed on the left and the floor feed on the right. The floor feed is the harder test. After the pulse, the standard replay washes out almost all the heterogeneity. It keeps only 0.05 kelvin, where the reference has 1.14. The extended replay keeps 0.61 kelvin, about half. Across all thermocouples, the mean error falls for both feeds. In a further test I nudge the runs, which means pulling them towards the measured temperatures. A nudged run is not a blind prediction. Even so, the nudged standard replay stays further from the rig than the nudged extended replay. So the standard replay keeps too little structure. These results lead to the conclusion of the thesis.

Answers

A non-passive scalar can be replayed if the correction acts at the thermocline and the database contains the flow regime, within stated limits.

Script

The discussion and conclusion chapters bring the five answers together. The answer to the central question is a careful yes. A recorded flow can carry a temperature that drives the flow. This holds if the correction acts at the thermocline and the database contains the flow regime. Five findings support this. No single face-exchange strength was best. The bounded correction restored the thermocline within the range of the data, and its direction mattered more than its strength. Under a cooled lid, the replay worked only with a database recorded under cooling. The filling error came from missing transport. In the vessel the operators transferred, but motion absent from the database stayed out of reach. These results come from one closed configuration, one mesh and one vessel. Within those limits the answer holds.

Future work

The correction strength is the first lead for learning, and none of these leads was tested in the thesis.

Script

Building on these limits, the outlook starts with the correction strength. I chose it by parameter studies, so it is the first candidate for a learned model. A small neural network could predict it locally from the replayed temperature field, inside the limiter, so conservation and bounds stay intact. The training could check the energy balance and use a few sensors. For motion missing from one database, a neural operator could take over the transport step. Or a sequence model, such as a Transformer or an xLSTM, could pick the next frame or database. None of these leads was tested in this thesis. A digital twin needs speed and sensor data. The replay is fast, and nudging is one way to bring in the sensors.
