Title

We record the flow once and ask how far the record can carry the heat on its own.

Good morning, and thank you for being here. My master's thesis is called Data-based Numerical Simulation of Buoyant Jet Flow. What you see is a small tank of liquid. A warm jet enters from the left, rises, and slowly builds a warm layer on top of the cold liquid. A full simulation of these ten minutes of flow takes more than nine hours. The idea of the thesis is easy to say. We record the flow once. Then we try to move the heat with that recording alone, in a few minutes. The question is how far this works when the heat itself drives the flow.

say your namesmall tank, warm jetnine hours for ten minutesrecord once, replay in minutesheat drives the flow

Perkenalan. Tangki kecil, jet hangat membangun lapisan. Simulasi penuh 9 jam lebih. Ide tesis: rekam alirannya sekali, lalu pindahkan panas dengan rekaman itu saja.

02

The database

Record the motion once, replay the heat many times.

The method is called recurrence CFD. First, we run one normal simulation in ANSYS Fluent. While it runs, we store six hundred frames, one for every second. A frame does not store the temperature. It stores where the content of every cell goes during that second. Later, a replay takes these frames one after the other and moves only the temperature. It never solves the flow equations again. That is why it is fast. The reference run took five hundred and sixty-nine minutes. One replay takes about two minutes on average. The price is that the motion is frozen. In this tank the warm water is lighter, so the temperature drives the motion. A frozen record cannot react to that, and that is the open question of the thesis.

one Fluent run600 frames, where each cell sends its contentreplay moves only temperature569 min vs about 2 minmotion is frozen

Cara kerja rCFD: satu run Fluent disimpan sebagai 600 frame perpindahan isi sel. Replay hanya memindahkan suhu, jadi cepat. Harganya: gerakan beku, padahal suhu menggerakkan aliran.

03

The question

Here the temperature moves the flow that carries it.

Recurrence CFD was developed for passive scalars. A passive scalar is carried by the flow, but it does not change the flow. Temperature in this tank is different. Warm water rises, cold water sinks, and so the temperature shapes its own transport. I test the method on this buoyant jet in two versions. On the left, the lid is adiabatic, so no heat leaves the tank. On the right, the lid loses heat, with a heat transfer coefficient of one hundred watts per square metre and kelvin. The questions follow this order. Can a replay keep the warm front as thin as the reference? Can it lose the right amount of heat through a cooled lid? And do the same tools work in a vessel of the laboratory rig?

passive scalar vs temperatureadiabatic lidcooled lid, h = 100three questions: front, lid heat, rig

rCFD dibuat untuk skalar pasif. Suhu di sini tidak pasif. Diuji dua versi: tutup adiabatik dan tutup didinginkan. Tiga pertanyaan: front tipis, panas lewat tutup, dan bejana rig.

04

Standard replay

One mixing constant cannot serve two measures.

I start with the standard replay and the adiabatic lid. The standard replay has one free number, the face exchange strength, f s d. It adds some mixing between neighbouring cells, because the recorded frames miss part of the small-scale mixing. I tested seven values, and the two measures do not agree. The mean temperature is best with little or no added mixing, at an error of about one kelvin. The height of the warm layer is best near zero point five. Why do they disagree? The outlet sits just below the warm front. When the replay smears the front, warm water reaches the outlet earlier and leaves the tank. So a numerical error at the front turns into an error in the heat balance, and one constant cannot fix both.

one free number, f_sdseven valuesmean T best with little mixinglayer height best near 0.5outlet just below the front

Replay standar punya satu konstanta pencampuran. Suhu rata-rata terbaik tanpa pencampuran tambahan, ketinggian lapisan terbaik di 0,5. Penyebabnya: outlet tepat di bawah front, front yang melebar menguras panas.

05

Extended replay

Sharpen the front, but never past its neighbours.

My answer is to act where the error is made, at the front. After each replay step, the extended replay applies a correction. It moves a little heat from the cold side to the warm side, up the local gradient. It works like diffusion run backwards, so it makes the front sharper. On its own, such a step is unstable, as you can see on the right. Without a limit, the values grow beyond the warmest and the coldest water in the data. So every exchange is limited, in the spirit of flux-corrected transport. No cell may leave the range of its neighbours. On the left, the front becomes sharp and every value stays inside the data range. A vertical weight points the exchange across the front. And because every exchange happens between two cells, the total heat stays the same.

act where the error is mademove heat up the gradientunstable without a limitlimiter: stay inside the neighbours' rangevertical weight, heat conserved

Koreksi setelah tiap langkah memindahkan panas melawan gradien sehingga front menajam. Tanpa batas, nilainya keluar rentang data. Dengan limiter gaya FCT, tiap sel tetap dalam rentang tetangganya. Bobot vertikal, panas total tetap.

06

One record, three fields

By the end of the run, only the corrected replay keeps the front thin.

Here are the three fields side by side, for the adiabatic case. On the left is the reference from Fluent, in the middle the standard replay, and on the right the extended replay with the correction. The timeline jumps between the six instants at which all three fields are stored. Watch the front, the thin band between the warm and the cold water. At the end of the run, the reference front is about five millimetres wide. The standard replay has smeared it to about sixteen. The extended replay brings it back to about five. The profile on the right shows the same thing. The extended curve follows the steep reference curve, while the standard curve is flat. Over the whole run, the error in the mean temperature is also more than halved.

reference, standard, extendedsix stored instantsfront about 5 mm vs about 16 mmsteep profiletrace error more than halved

Tiga medan berdampingan. Di akhir run, front referensi sekitar 5 mm, replay standar melebar sampai sekitar 16 mm, extended replay kembali sekitar 5 mm. Galat suhu rata-rata turun lebih dari separuh.

07

Cooled lid

The recording must hold the cooling.

Now the lid loses heat. Heat can only leave through the lid as fast as the motion brings warm water up to it. When the lid cools the water below it, that water becomes heavy and sinks, and warm water takes its place. A recording made with an adiabatic lid has never seen this motion. So with the adiabatic database, the replay removes only sixty-three per cent of the heat that the reference loses through the lid. More mixing does not recover it, and the correction does not recover it either. When we record the database with the cooling switched on, the standard replay already removes ninety-eight per cent. The database must match the flow regime, not only the case.

heat leaves as fast as motion brings warm watercooled water sinksadiabatic database: 63 %mixing or correction do not helpcooled database: 98 %

Panas keluar lewat tutup hanya secepat gerakan membawa air hangat ke atas. Rekaman adiabatik tidak memuat gerakan itu: hanya 63 %. Dengan basis data yang direkam saat pendinginan: 98 %.

08

Correction under cooling

With the correction, heat ends up in the right place, not only in the right amount.

With the right database, the amount of heat is almost right. But the standard replay still puts the heat in the wrong place. Its end profile is two point eight kelvin away from the reference, as a root mean square. The top layer is too cold and the bulk below is too warm. With the correction at its working point, s equal to zero point five five, the profile error falls to zero point four one kelvin. The mean temperature at the end is within zero point zero six kelvin of the reference, on a cooling depth of about nine and a half kelvin. Through the lid, the corrected replay removes slightly more heat than the reference, about one hundred and two per cent. I chose the working point by the end profile and the end temperature.

right amount, wrong placestandard profile 2.8 Ktop too cold, bulk too warms = 0.55: profile 0.41 Kend within 0.06 K, lid 102 %

Dengan basis data yang benar, jumlah panas hampir benar tetapi letaknya salah (profil 2,8 K). Dengan koreksi s = 0,55: profil 0,41 K, suhu akhir dalam 0,06 K dari referensi. Panas lewat tutup 102 %.

09

What stays open

While the tank fills, the layers separate too slowly.

One error survives every local correction. While the warm water flows in, the reference separates the layers quickly, and the replay separates them too slowly. In the heterogeneity of the field, the gap is two point two five kelvin during the warm inflow. No local rule moves this error. So I tested a different idea. The recording also holds a vertical convective heat flux. When the replay adds this recorded flux, the error drops to zero point four five kelvin, with no fitted number. That tells us the missing part is transport. But the flux is a test, not a finished tool. The tank then ends zero point eight six kelvin too cold, and the flux field belongs to the run that recorded it.

one error survives2.25 K during warm inflowno local rule moves itrecorded vertical flux: 0.45 Ka test, ends 0.86 K too cold

Satu galat bertahan: lapisan memisah terlalu lambat saat tangki terisi (2,25 K). Fluks vertikal yang direkam menurunkannya ke 0,45 K tanpa angka fit, jadi yang hilang adalah transport. Tapi itu uji, bukan komponen: tangki berakhir 0,86 K terlalu dingin.

10

Laboratory vessel

The operators carry to the rig, the missing motion does not.

The last part leaves the closed tank. The department has a laboratory rig with a through-flow vessel of seven and a half litres. A warm pulse enters either through the lid line or through the floor line, and the water leaves through a drain at half height. Seven thermocouples measure the temperature. I recorded one database in the lid-fed case and replayed both cases from it. Against its own reference simulation, the extended replay lowers the mean error over the seven heights from one point two eight to zero point seven seven kelvin with the lid feed, and from one point six one to zero point six eight with the floor feed. But look at the floor feed. In the reference, the warm water rises along the wall in plumes. The lid-fed recording has never seen this motion, so no replay can create it. The extended replay keeps sharp structures, but in other places.

7.5-litre through-flow vessellid feed or floor feed, one lid-fed database1.28 to 0.77 K, 1.61 to 0.68 Kfloor plumes along the wallreplay cannot create them

Bejana rig 7,5 liter, diumpan dari tutup atau dasar, keduanya di-replay dari satu basis data umpan tutup. Extended replay menurunkan galat (1,28 ke 0,77 K dan 1,61 ke 0,68 K), tetapi plume dasar tidak bisa diciptakan karena tidak ada di rekaman.

11

Conclusions

Act where the error is made, and match the database to the regime.

Four points summarise the thesis. First, act where the error is made. A bounded correction across the front removes the trade-off of one mixing constant. Second, a front error becomes an energy error. When an outlet sits near the front, smearing drains heat, and the same correction repairs both. Third, the database has to match the flow regime, not only the case. For a temperature that drives the flow, a boundary condition can switch on a new regime by itself. Fourth, the correction carries to an open vessel as a set of bounded operators, but it cannot supply motion that the record does not hold. These statements come from one tank, one mesh and one vessel. The references are simulations, and the speed-up of about two hundred and sixty times counts only the replay, not the recording run.

1 act at the front2 front error = energy error near an outlet3 match the regime4 operators carry, motion does notlimits: simulations, one mesh, replay-only speed-up

Empat kesimpulan: koreksi di front menghapus tarik-ulur satu konstanta, galat front menjadi galat energi dekat outlet, basis data harus sesuai rezim aliran, dan operator bisa dibawa ke bejana, gerakan yang hilang tidak.

12

Where learning fits

Two errors are left, and both are learning problems.

Two problems are left, and both are learning problems. The correction strength is still chosen by a sweep, case by case. And the replay cannot create motion that the database does not hold. A recurrence database is a good place for learning, because it holds many states of one flow, and a trained model is cheap to run. The first step I would take is small. A small network predicts the local correction strength from the replayed field, and it acts inside the limiter, so the bounds and the conservation stay. A neural operator could learn the step from one field to the next. And a sequence model, such as a transformer or an xLSTM, could choose which frame, or which database, to play next. None of these models was tried in this thesis.

strength chosen by sweepsmissing motionlearned strength inside the limiterneural operatorsequence model picks framesnot tried here

Dua masalah tersisa: kekuatan koreksi masih dipilih dengan sapuan, dan gerakan yang tidak ada di rekaman. Langkah pertama: jaringan kecil memprediksi kekuatan lokal di dalam limiter. Lalu neural operator dan model urutan. Belum dicoba di tesis.
